Odoo Chess

Multiplayer Chess Game for Odoo

Challenge Your Colleagues to a Game of Chess!

A fully integrated multiplayer chess game that runs entirely inside Odoo. No external services required!

Key Features

Real-Time Multiplayer

Challenge any Odoo user to a chess game. See moves update in real-time using Odoo's longpolling bus.

Elo Rating System

Track your chess skill with a proper Elo rating. Compete on the leaderboard to become the office champion!

Play vs Bots

Practice against AI opponents of various difficulty levels, from random moves to strategic play.

Game Stakes

Add fun stakes to your games like "Loser buys coffee" to make matches more interesting!

Full Game Controls

Resign, offer draws, claim draws by repetition - all standard chess controls available.

Game Persistence

Games are saved in the database. Resume disconnected games anytime!

How It Works

  1. Create a Game: Click "New Game" and choose to play against a human or bot.
  2. Send Invitation: For human games, your opponent receives a notification.
  3. Play Chess: Once accepted, play with drag-and-drop piece movement.
  4. Win or Draw: Checkmate your opponent, agree to a draw, or resign if needed.
  5. Track Progress: Watch your rating change and climb the leaderboard!

Requirements

  • Odoo 18 or 19 (Community or Enterprise)
  • python-chess library: pip install python-chess
  • chessboard.js library (included, or download from chessboardjs.com)
